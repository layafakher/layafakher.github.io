Research & Publications

Selected Works

Peer-reviewed publications and submissions spanning structured LLM inference, mental health NLP, multimodal benchmarking, and synthetic data.

01
IEEE · 2025

Retrieval and Citation-Augmented Generation for Explainable Detection of Anorexia on Social Media

Comparative study of RAG and CAG frameworks for explainable anorexia detection using six state-of-the-art LLMs including GPT-5, LLaMA 3.1, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, and DeepSeek V3.
02
ECML PKDD 2026 · 2025

Robust Mental Health Detection via Structured LLM Inference: A Study of LangGraph and Chain-of-Verification

LangGraph-based structured inference framework improving robustness and interpretability for early anorexia and depression detection. Evaluates a Chain-of-Verification (CoVe) baseline for self-verification in clinical LLMs.

03
ICPE 2026 · 2025

To See or to Hear First: The Energy Cost of Prompting Order

First systematic study of how prompting order (caption-first vs. image-first) affects accuracy, latency, and GPU energy consumption in multimodal LLMs. Six MLLMs evaluated across ScienceQA, ChartQA, AI2D, MMMU, and OCR-VQA using NVML power profiling.

04
JAIR · 2025

Synthetic Data Generation in the Era of Large Language Models: A Comprehensive Survey

Systematic taxonomy of synthetic data generation techniques — prompting, diffusion, and multi-agent — with analysis of evaluation frameworks, ethical considerations, and reproducibility challenges.

05
IEEE ICMI 2025 · 2025

Harnessing Large Language Models for Early Risk Assessment of Anorexia on Social Media

Fine-tuned BERT, RoBERTa, DistilBERT, MentalBERT, ClinicalBERT, and GPT-2 on the CLEF eRisk 2024 dataset for early anorexia risk classification with high sensitivity.

 06
IEEE CSI AISP 2024 · 2024

Depression Detection on eRisk 2017 using Long Short-term Memory Models

LSTM-based recurrent neural networks applied to the CLEF eRisk 2017 dataset for early detection of depression from chronologically ordered social media posts.
