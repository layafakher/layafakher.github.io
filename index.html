Current Projects

In preparationAug 2026 – Present

RAPPO: Towards Efficient and Adaptive Graph-Based Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Studies retrieval strategies for GraphRAG that balance accuracy, latency, and compute cost. Benchmarked RAG, RAPTOR, HippoRAG, and HippoRAG2 on 2,062 medical questions, and profiled inference latency, token use, graph storage, and indexing cost to find where retrieval pipelines can be made faster and cheaper.

In preparationJun 2026 – Present

Topology, Scale, and Efficiency: Benchmarking Agentic LLM Pipelines on Longitudinal Synthetic Clinical Data

A benchmark of GPU energy across agentic pipeline designs, where earlier energy studies profile single models and clinical multi-agent studies report only tokens, latency, and accuracy. Compares four CrewAI topologies (single pass, sequential chain, chain with a validating retry loop, and parallel workers with an aggregator) on clinical complication prediction, using Synthea FHIR cohorts with 2, 5, and 10 years of history.
